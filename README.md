# Sales Performance & Financial Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an **Excel-based business and financial analysis** of AtliQ Hardware. The project focuses on analyzing sales, cost of goods sold (COGS), gross margin, gross margin percentage (GM%), market performance, and performance against targets.

The analysis covers multiple fiscal years and markets to identify business trends, compare regional performance, and understand profitability.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze sales and financial performance across fiscal years.
* Compare **Net Sales, COGS, Gross Margin, and GM%**.
* Analyze market-wise sales and profitability.
* Compare actual market performance against targets.
* Identify trends and performance differences across regions and sub-zones.
* Present business insights through Excel reports and visualizations.

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Net Sales increased from **$87.48M in FY2019** to **$196.69M in FY2020** and **$598.88M in FY2021**.
* Gross Margin increased from **$36.24M in FY2019** to **$73.32M in FY2020** and **$218.16M in FY2021**.
* Overall GM% was **41.43% in FY2019, 37.28% in FY2020, and 36.43% in FY2021**.
* India recorded **$161.26M Net Sales in FY2021**, followed by the USA at **$87.78M**.
* The market performance analysis shows an overall **FY2021 target variance of -$54.9M (-9.2%)**.
* Market and sub-zone analysis helps identify differences in sales contribution and gross-margin performance.
